# BUG-101: Customer got 35% off with two coupons

A customer entered the coupons SAVE20 (20% off) and WELCOME15 (15% off) on an order with a
subtotal of $100.00 and was charged $65.00. Support says only one percentage coupon should apply per
order, so the total should have been $80.00. Reproduced twice. Fixed-amount coupons look fine.
